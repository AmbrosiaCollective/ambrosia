# Community conduct

Ambrosia welcomes patient, evidence-based collaboration. Treat people with
respect, explain technical disagreements clearly and help newcomers find a
manageable task. Critique the work without attacking its author.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sharing private information, spam and
repeatedly disruptive behavior are not welcome. Respect other contributors'
time: coordinate work, avoid duplicate submissions and keep each PR focused.

Respect privacy and project boundaries. Do not share credentials, game files,
keys or game-derived assets. Ask before sharing another person's private report
or conversation.

Report conduct concerns privately to a moderator or maintainer through the
[community](https://discord.gg/Bdguf59NvB). Maintainers may remove inappropriate
content, limit participation or revoke access. Moderation decisions should be
proportionate, consistent and handled without exposing the reporter publicly.
